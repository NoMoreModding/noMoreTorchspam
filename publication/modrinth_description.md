## ![logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoMoreModding/noMoreTorchspam/master/src/main/resources/logo.png) 

A small mod that adds the Glow Crystal, a scalable light source. 

 <p style="padding-left: 30px;">Have you ever wanted to light up a larger area?</p><p style="padding-left: 30px;">Before this mod you would probably spam torches all over but spam no more!</p><p style="padding-left: 30px;">The glow crystal will save you :3</p> 

You can place up to 4 crystals at once to get the maximum light range of about 50 blocks!
